Everything you need, under one roof

From routine blood tests to ultrasound scans and full health check-ups, everything you need to understand your health is under one roof in Awada, Onitsha.

Haematology

Blood counts, anaemia screening, malaria parasite tests and more, analysed with care so your doctor gets dependable answers.

Clinical chemistry

Liver, kidney, blood sugar and cholesterol profiles run on calibrated analysers, with quality checks at every step.

Microbiology

Urine, stool and swab tests, cultures and sensitivity, examined by trained microbiologists to guide the right treatment.

Ultrasound scan

Comfortable, private ultrasound scans for abdominal, pelvic and obstetric needs, with clear reports for your doctor.

Health check

Wellness, pre-employment, pre-marital and antenatal screening packages that give you a clear picture of your health.
